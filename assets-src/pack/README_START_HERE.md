# Neuro Pharmacy — independent concept asset pack

Created 19 October 2026 for Arthur Kitandwe's portfolio/pitch development. This pack supplies visual assets, demo catalogue data and handoff notes, not a website implementation.

Includes 16 independent AI-generated image masters and 16 smaller web versions, proposed SVG logo variants, monogram and favicons, 12 interface icons, three empty/completion states, a fictional prescription-process sample, two social graphics and structured demo data.

Open ASSET_INDEX.html and CONTACT_SHEET.jpg. Read guides/AGENT_HANDOFF.md, guides/CONTENT_REGISTER.md and guides/VISUAL_NOTES.md before implementation. Exact archive inventory and hashes are in asset-manifest.json.

All people, pharmacy interiors, storefronts, delivery riders, packaging and products are fictional concept artwork. They are not real staff portraits, verified premises, Neuro inventory or proof of delivery services. Branding is proposed, not the official logo. Image text is illustrative; important website text must be live HTML.

Full-dimension WebP masters are supplied; original generation PNGs are not duplicated. The source prompts are included. Catalogue prices and delivery fees are explicitly demo data. No medical advice, drug doses, prescription medicine listings or real prescription documents are supplied.
